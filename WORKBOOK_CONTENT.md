# PART 12 — Week 12 Final Project Workbook Content

**Project Title:** StudyFlow Planner – Student Study Planning & Task Management System

**Problem Statement:**
Students often struggle to organize multiple subjects, track assignments, and plan consistent
study time, leading to missed deadlines and uneven exam preparation. StudyFlow Planner solves
this by providing a single, easy-to-use full stack web application where a student can manage
subjects, schedule study sessions, track tasks, and visualize overall study progress in one
place.

**Objectives:**
- Provide a centralized dashboard summarizing a student's academic workload
- Allow full CRUD management of subjects, tasks, and study plans
- Help students plan study sessions by date, time, and topic
- Track completion progress at both the subject and overall level
- Provide search and filtering to quickly find relevant tasks or subjects
- Build the application using a real, industry-relevant full stack (MERN) architecture

**Technologies Used:**
- React.js, Vite, React Router DOM, Axios (Frontend)
- Node.js, Express.js (Backend)
- MongoDB, Mongoose (Database)
- Git & GitHub (Version Control)
- Postman (API Testing)

**Feature 01: Dashboard**
- Purpose: Give the student an at-a-glance summary of their academic workload
- How it works: Aggregates counts from the Subject and Task collections via `GET /api/dashboard`
- Important implementation: `dashboardController.js` calculates total/completed/pending tasks,
  today's tasks, and overall progress percentage
- Screenshot required: Dashboard page with populated statistics

**Feature 02: Subject Management**
- Purpose: Let students organize their coursework by subject
- How it works: Full CRUD via `/api/subjects`; deleting a subject cascades to remove its tasks/plans
- Important implementation: `Subject` Mongoose schema with progress and study-hour tracking
- Screenshot required: Subjects page showing subject cards with progress bars

**Feature 03: Task Management**
- Purpose: Track individual assignments/study tasks with due dates and priority
- How it works: Full CRUD via `/api/tasks`, with status toggle and status/priority/search filters
- Important implementation: `taskController.js` query-parameter-based filtering
- Screenshot required: Tasks page with filters applied and a completed task shown struck-through

**Feature 04: Study Planner & Calendar**
- Purpose: Let students schedule specific study sessions by date and time
- How it works: `StudyPlan` model links to a Subject; Calendar page groups plans by date
- Important implementation: Time validation (`endTime` must be after `startTime`) in `StudyPlanForm.jsx`
- Screenshot required: Study Planner page and Calendar page showing a scheduled session

**Feature 05: Progress Tracking**
- Purpose: Visualize overall and subject-wise study progress
- How it works: Combines subject `progress` fields and task completion ratios into progress bars
- Important implementation: `Progress.jsx` page using `useMemo` for derived statistics
- Screenshot required: Progress page showing subject-wise progress bars
